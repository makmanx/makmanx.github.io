Supporting library

What is established

The snapshot contains 255 mathematical source modules and six auxiliary audit modules. Its 1,052 counted supporting declarations include eight attributed existing proofs. These counts are not percentages of manuscript coverage.

  • Interior continuous representatives for actual constant-source weak solutions for every p > 1 and dimension n ≥ 2.
  • Smooth representatives in the linear case p = 2, including dimension two.
  • Variational, Sobolev, comparison and capacity foundations with their stated boundary and exponent restrictions.
  • Weak exterior Green-function existence for p > n and every nonempty compact set, with continuous zero boundary values and a bounded canonical-profile remainder.

General nonlinear gradient Hölder regularity, general critical-point viscosity comparison, critical p = n Green existence, finite asymptotic constants and the remaining arguments for A, B and C are open in this release.

Reproducibility

Checking the formal statements

The project is pinned to Lean 4.33.1 and an exact Mathlib commit. Its audit accepts only the standard logical axioms and rejects sorryAx or custom mathematical axioms. Hosting the files on GitHub makes them available; the Lean kernel checks the proofs when the project is built.

The development used AI assistance. Matching a formal statement to its intended mathematical meaning remains a separate review task. This release does not claim independent human mathematical review or complete formal verification of the manuscript.
