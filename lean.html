Brunn–Minkowski inequalities for p-capacity of compact sets

This Lean 4 library accompanies the preprint Brunn–Minkowski inequality for p-capacity of compact sets (arXiv:2610.28421). Theorems A(a), A(c), B(c), C(a), C(b), C(c), Proposition 4.8 (prop7.3), Lemma 4.7 (lem7.2), and Lemma 3.6 (lem3.7) are completely formalized in Lean 4 for all exponents 1 < p < ∞ and dimensions n ≥ 2, with zero sorrys and only standard logical axioms (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound).

Formalized results

What is established

All main theorems below are checked by the Lean 4.33.1 kernel with zero sorrys and standard axioms only.

  • Theorem A(a) & A(c): Brunn–Minkowski inequalities for variational p-capacity (1 < p < n) and supercritical Green capacity (p > n) of arbitrary nonempty compact sets in ℝn, n ≥ 2.
  • Theorem B(c): Brunn–Minkowski inequality for the critical outer capacity (p = n ≥ 2) of arbitrary nonempty compact sets.
  • Theorem C(a), C(b), C(c): Pointwise capacitary and Green's-function min/max comparisons for p-regular sets and outer regularizations across all 1 < p < ∞, n ≥ 2.
  • Nonlinear viscosity engine: Alexandrov's theorem (from Rademacher + Minty resolvent), local Jensen's lemma, JLM viscosity comparison (Lemma 3.6 / lem3.7), nonlinear sup-min envelope viscosity subsolution (Lemma 4.7 / lem7.2), and Proposition 4.8 (prop7.3).

Reproducibility

Checking the formal statements

The project is pinned to Lean 4.33.1 and an exact Mathlib commit. Its audit accepts only the standard logical axioms and rejects sorryAx or custom mathematical axioms. Hosting the files on GitHub makes them available; the Lean kernel checks the proofs when the project is built.

The development used AI assistance. Matching a formal statement to its intended mathematical meaning remains a separate review task. This release does not claim independent human mathematical review or complete formal verification of the manuscript.
