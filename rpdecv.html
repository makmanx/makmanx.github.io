Regularity Theory of PDEs & Calculus of Variations on Domains with Rough Boundaries

Spring Eastern Sectional Meeting
13–14 April 2019 · University of Connecticut Hartford, Hartford, Connecticut

Official AMS programme ↗

The session

PDEs, geometry & variations

This session focused on elliptic and parabolic PDEs and the calculus of variations on domains with rough boundaries. Topics included second- and higher-order elliptic equations and systems in non-smooth media, free boundary problems, harmonic and nonlinear elliptic measures, analysis on uniformly rectifiable sets, and regularity of free boundaries.

The meeting connected researchers in PDEs, harmonic analysis, geometric measure theory and the calculus of variations, with a particular focus on graduate students, early-career mathematicians and future collaborations.

Organisers: Murat Akman and Zihui Zhao.

Original meeting details

The abstract submission deadline was 5 February 2019. Meeting number: 1148; session: SS 5A — Regularity Theory of PDEs and Calculus of Variations on Domains with Rough Boundaries. AMS abstract submission system.
