Regularity of PDEs on Rough Domains

Spring Eastern Sectional Meeting
21–22 April 2018 · Northeastern University, Boston, Massachusetts

Official AMS programme ↗

The session

Analysis at rough boundaries

This session brought together work on regularity of elliptic and parabolic partial differential equations on rough domains: solvability of second- and higher-order elliptic equations and systems in non-smooth media, free boundary problems, harmonic and nonlinear elliptic measures, and analysis on uniformly rectifiable sets.

It aimed to connect researchers in PDEs, harmonic analysis and geometric measure theory, with a particular focus on graduate students and early-career mathematicians.

Organisers: Murat Akman and Max Engelstein.

Original meeting details

The abstract submission deadline was 20 February 2018. Meeting number: 1139; session: SS 14A — Regularity of PDEs on Rough Domains. AMS abstract submission system.

Session organiser contacts at the time: murat.akman “at” uconn.edu and maxe “at” mit.edu.
